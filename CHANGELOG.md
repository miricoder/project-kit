# Changelog

What changed in each version of Dumpit. The app shows the entries a device has not seen
yet after it updates ("Updated to …"), so write them for the person using the app.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added
- Type or dictate anything; it is filed as a task, an expense, your monthly budget, or a note
- Sign in with a link sent to your email; the same data on your phone and your computer
- Install it from the browser to your Home Screen; captures made offline are filed when you are back online
- After an update, the app says what changed
