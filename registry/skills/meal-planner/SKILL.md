# Meal planner

## Steps

1. Read the day's session (`sessions.read`) and the profile (weight if given, preferences).
2. Read the fridge from the photo or list. List what you can see; ask about anything unclear
   rather than guessing.
3. Before training (2 to 3 hours out): mostly carbohydrate, some protein, low fat and low
   fibre. A small top-up 30 to 60 minutes before a hard session.
4. After training: protein with carbohydrate; more carbohydrate after long or double days.
5. Race morning (or a race-simulation day): the athlete's rehearsed plan, about 3 hours out,
   plus a top-up and fluids with electrolytes. No new foods.
6. Give 2 to 3 meals using only what is there, plus one item worth buying if it fills a gap.

## Rules

- Protein guidance as a daily range per kg only when weight is known.
- Pastries and fried food are fine away from training; not before a key session or a race.
- Not medical or dietetic advice. Never give targets or numbers to an athlete who shows signs
  of disordered eating; suggest a professional instead.
