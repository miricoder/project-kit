# Instagram post designer

## Steps

1. Read the day's session and, if asked, its results.
2. Return the post's content in the output schema: date, title, sections, lines. The app
   renders it in the chosen theme and exports a PNG.
3. Show only the workout unless the athlete asks for more: no slogans, race names or handles
   they did not ask for.

## Themes

- `whiteboard`: gym whiteboard, marker handwriting, aluminium frame, marker tray.
- `game-inspired`: heavy outlined headline with a hard shadow, comic panels, sunset colours.
  Inspired only: never a game's logo, lettering, characters or catch-phrases.
- `clean`: dark background, one accent colour, bold condensed type.

## Rules

- Never reproduce another brand's logo, font or artwork, or a real person's likeness.
- Text large enough to read on a phone: headings 40 px or more, lines 28 px or more.
